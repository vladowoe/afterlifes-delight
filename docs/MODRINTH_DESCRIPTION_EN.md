# Afterlife's Delight

**Even falling into lava counts as a hunger-induced faint here — one so severe that your soul leaves your body.**

Afterlife's Delight is a Farmer's Delight addon that turns returning to life into a culinary challenge.

After pressing Respawn, you awaken near the place where you died as an incorporeal ghost. Mobs no longer notice you, ordinary items pass through your hands, and you cannot fight, break blocks, or use the material world. To regain your body, your soul needs food prepared in advance.

## Features

- Your ghostly state persists through relogs and world restarts. Your soul appears at a safe location near where you died, even if it happened in another dimension.
- The properties of four supernatural stews depend on how you combine otherworldly seasonings. A dish can resurrect you where you stand, follow your soul through death, return it to where you died, or combine both effects. Every stew fully restores health and hunger.
- A new tool, the pestle, grinds ingredients on Farmer's Delight's Cutting Board. Its material determines durability and available recipes.
- The spirit world changes more than the rules: a cold spectral filter, fog, particles, ambience, a translucent body, and an echo trail follow your ghost until resurrection. Flight, sounds, and atmosphere intensity are configurable.

Living players can also eat any of these stews as ordinary food, but a dish's otherworldly properties only awaken after the soul has left the body.

## Compatibility

The mod works without `keepInventory` and has been tested with Corpse, GraveStone Mod, Sophisticated Backpacks stored inside corpses and graves, as well as Sable: Ragdoll Corpse and Sable's physical ragdolls.

Ghosts cannot recover ordinary belongings before resurrection, while stews made to follow the soul are transferred without duplication. A ghost can briefly grab its physical corpse, but it will keep slipping from spectral hands.

## Requirements

- Minecraft 1.21.1
- NeoForge 21.1.248+
- Farmer's Delight 1.3.2+
- Java 21
