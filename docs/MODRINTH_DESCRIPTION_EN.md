# Afterlife's Delight

**A Farmer's Delight add-on for Hardcore worlds where death is only the start of dinner.**

Afterlife's Delight was made for playing Hardcore with friends. In vanilla, one unlucky creeper can leave a friend watching everyone else play. Here, a fallen player returns as a ghost while the group gets a chance to cook them back to life.

After pressing Respawn, the soul appears safely near its body. Mobs ignore ghosts, and ghosts cannot fight, break blocks, or collect ordinary items. Returning to life requires special afterlife food: a stew or apple pie prepared by a living cook, or seasoned in advance so it follows its owner through death.

Burned in lava? You were apparently so hungry that your soul left your body.

If everyone becomes a ghost and nobody has the right food, no one is left to use the Cooking Pot. The world stays quiet until a new cook joins the server.

## What the mod adds

- Four resurrection effects, each available as a stew and an apple pie.
- Mix-and-match seasonings determine whether a dish follows the soul through death and whether it returns the ghost to the place they died.
- Whole pies can be placed, divided into four servings, carried through death, and shared between living players and ghosts.
- Pestles made from different materials grind Echo Shards, Chorus Fruit, and Netherite Scrap on the Farmer's Delight Cutting Board.
- A cold filter, fog, particles, ambience, a translucent body, an echo trail, and camera sway shape the spirit world.
- Ghost state persists through relogs and full game restarts. Flight, sounds, atmosphere, and interaction with physical corpses are configurable.

Every resurrection dish restores health and hunger completely. Living players can eat stews and pie servings as ordinary food; their supernatural effects only apply to ghosts.

## Compatibility

Afterlife's Delight works without \`keepInventory\` and has been tested with Corpse, GraveStone Mod, Sophisticated Backpacks stored inside corpses and graves, Sable: Ragdoll Corpse, and Sable's physical ragdolls.

Ghosts cannot recover ordinary belongings before resurrection. Afterlife food prepared to follow the soul is transferred without duplication. When physical ragdolls are installed, a corpse may slip from a ghost's hands; the chance and pickup cooldown are configurable.

## Requirements
